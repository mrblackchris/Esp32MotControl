Local controls

Run switch: GPIO32 to GND. Direction switch: GPIO33 to GND (closed = reverse). Potentiometer: ends to 3.3 V and GND, wiper to GPIO34. Range: 0–50 electrical Hz.

After selecting Local or issuing Stop, switch OFF then ON to start. Direction changes while running stop the outputs and require OFF then ON. If zero frequency stops the motor, cycle the run switch to restart.

Modulation remains at the applied setting in Local mode. This is open-loop waveform control with independent frequency and modulation, without a V/f profile, acceleration ramp, current feedback or speed measurement. Stop disables the six motor gate signals; GPIO25 continues independently.
